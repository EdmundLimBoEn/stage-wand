# Stage Wand privacy policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Stage Wand is a presentation remote. The phone app (iOS and Android) controls slides and the pointer on a computer you own, which runs the Stage Wand host (macOS, Windows or Linux).

**What the app stores.** Your pairing code, the host address you typed or scanned, your pointer sensitivity, pointer mode and transport choice. These stay on your phone in the app's local preferences. Deleting the app deletes them.

**What the app sends, and where.** Only to the computer you pair with: the pairing code (to authenticate) and your control commands (next/previous slide, pointer movement, clicks, scrolls, keys). They go directly over your local network or Bluetooth. If you choose to use a tunnel URL that you set up yourself (for example Cloudflare Tunnel), traffic goes through that provider under its terms.

**What we collect.** Nothing. Stage Wand has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs, and no developer-operated server. We never receive your commands, codes or addresses.

**Permissions.** Bluetooth and Local Network access are used only to find and talk to your host computer. The app plays silent audio in the background so the hardware volume buttons can keep controlling slides while the phone is locked during a presentation; it records nothing.

**TestFlight.** If you test a beta through Apple TestFlight, Apple shares the crash reports, feedback and screenshots you choose to send, and basic device/usage information, with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. They are used only to fix the app.

**Children.** Stage Wand is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.
